What I am building: Grothiv

Grothiv is a startup collaboration platform and co-founder matching network for people who want to move from an idea to an active team.

Find your co-founder and build it together.

Grothiv helps founders, developers, designers, marketers, and other builders discover complementary people based on skills, goals, timezone, and working style. The goal is simple: make it easier to find the right collaborators, form a team, and launch faster together.

How it works

  • Post an idea: Share a vision and attract builders who want to contribute.
  • Get matched: Discover people with complementary skills and aligned goals.
  • Form a team: Align on expectations and formalize the partnership.
  • Build together: Use shared workspaces, real-time chat, task boards, and milestones to ship.

Why Grothiv exists

The collaboration loop stays free: posting an idea, finding a match, forming a team, and building together should not be hidden behind a paywall before a network has liquidity.

Grothiv is for early-stage founders and builders who are ready to stop waiting and start shipping.
